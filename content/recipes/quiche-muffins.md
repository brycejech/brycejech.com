Pre-heat oven to 400°F

Cook sausage, peppers, and onions in skillet.

Poach spinache.

Whisk eggs with seasonings in bowl then combine with other ingredients.

Evenly distribute into greased XL muffin tray.

Bake 30 minutes.

**Note**: These will increase 20-30% in size when cooking. Do not fill trays completely or it will overflow while baking.
