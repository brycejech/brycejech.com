Pre-heat oven to 350°F.

Boil noods al dente in salted water and drain. They will cook further when baked, be sure not to fully cook them here.

Make roux with flour and butter.

Slowly add heavy cream and mix roux.

Add evaporated milk, dijon, and seasonings.

Add cheese to make sauce, reserve half for layering/topping.

Mix the sauce with the noods.

Add to baking dish with a layer of cheese in the middle and top with final layer of cheese.

Bake at 350°F until done.
