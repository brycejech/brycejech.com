Peel potatoes, cut into desired fry size and rinse. Par boil fries in salted water, drain, pat dry, and allow to air dry. Par boiling is so that when frying we are just cooking the batter to desired doneness. If par boiling went too far, bag and put in freezer.

Prepare batter and heat oil to 350 while drying/freezing. Once prepared, dredge fries in batter then fry. Add salt after frying if desired. Serve immediately.
