Preheat oven to 350°F.

Combine shortening, both sugars, sugar and water in a large bowl or stand mixer.

Add 4 eggs (one at a time) and the baking soda and salt and mix well.

Add 3 cups of flour (one at a time) and mix well.

Add the chocolate chips and remaining flour and mix a final time.

Bake for 9 - 10 minutes.
