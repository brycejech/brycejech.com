Pre-heat oven to 350°F.

Cut asparagus to desired length and rub in olive oil until covered.

Lay evenly on a baking sheet with raised edges (line with foil for easier clean up).

Cover with copped thyme and rosemary.

Sprinkle with lemon juice, salt and pepper as desired.

Bake until half cooked, remove and add balsamic vinegar.

When nearly done, remove and add parmesean (or other stinky cheese) and broil until done (2-3 minutes).

**Note**: Can also use a balsamic glaze if sweeter dish is preferred. A glaze will cook faster, so it should be added later in the cooking process.
