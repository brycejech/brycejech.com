Makes 3 quarts of liquid.

Cut tops off okra or poke holes with toothpick before adding to jars so that they fill with liquid and don't float.

Distribute mustard seed evenly across jars.

Pack the okra tightly and cover with liquid.

Seal jars and leave for at least 3 weeks before eating.
