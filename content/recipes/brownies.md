Pre-heat oven to 325°F.

Spray 8x8 baking dish with cooking spray and line with parchment paper (do not use 9x9 dish or brownies will over cook). Spray the parchment paper.

Combine dry ingredients in a medium bowl.

In a large bowl, whisk the eggs, add olive oil, water, and vanilla then mix.

Slowly add the dry ingredients to the large bowl and mix until combined.

Pour the batter into the pan and smooth it out with a spatula. It will be thick, that's ok.

Bake for 40 - 50 minutes or until toothpick comes out with only a few crumbs attached.

**Note**: It is far better to pull the brownies early than to leave them too long.

Let cool 2 hours before serving.
